How this site is made

Status

This is a PhD research project in its first phase. The site shows the research question, the research design and a literature map. It contains no experimental results. Drafts and results will appear here only after they exist and have been checked.

How papers enter the map

  • Candidate papers are found by topic-specific searches.
  • Each paper is looked up by DOI in Crossref or by identifier in the arXiv API. A paper counts as verified only when the title, first author and year of that record match the catalog row. The lookup is logged with its time.
  • Summaries are short paraphrases. They are not copied abstracts and they have not yet been audited by an independent reviewer.
  • Open-access PDF locations come from OpenAlex and arXiv. PDFs are not hosted on this site; follow the links to the publisher or the preprint server.

Use of AI

The literature search, the verification scripts, the summaries and this site were produced with assistance from AI models (Anthropic Claude, run through the OpenCode agent tool) under the direction of the project owner. Every catalog entry carries its verification state, and nothing is presented as a finding before an independent check.
